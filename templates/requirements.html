Before You Report, Adopt, or Volunteer

Please keep these requirements in mind so our team can respond faster and place animals safely.

Report an Animal Back to Home

Reporting a Rescue

  • Share a clear location and nearby landmark.
  • Describe visible injuries or urgent risks.
  • Include a working phone number for follow-up.

Adoption Basics

  • Use a valid email and phone number.
  • Be ready for a short screening call.
  • Confirm your home is prepared for long-term care.

Volunteer Readiness

  • Choose your available area and timing accurately.
  • List any transport, foster, or medical support skills.
  • Respond quickly when a case is assigned.
